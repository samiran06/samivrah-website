03 — A few good questions

Before you tune in.

What is FORM?

FORM is a fictional premium audio brand created for Samiran Koley’s UI/UX portfolio. Product imagery, colours and prices are illustrative design concepts, not real product offers.

Can I try the shopping experience?

Yes. Preview colours, add a pair to your bag, adjust quantities and remove items. The bag stays in this browser when you revisit. No payment or real order is processed.

What guided the design?

A clear path from discovery to selection. Large product imagery, a restrained colour palette, readable product choices and a simple bag keep attention on the product.

Who designed this concept?

Creative direction by Samiran Koley for Samivrah. An independent exploration of interactive commerce, created with AI-assisted development and generated product imagery.
