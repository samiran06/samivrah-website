Important information

Custom work needs a clear scope

Is every solution priced the same?

No. Pricing depends on users, features, data, security, integrations, hosting and support requirements.

Are domain and hosting included?

Domain, hosting, paid tools, external APIs and third-party subscriptions are separate unless included in writing.

Can you replace a full ERP?

The service is intended for focused operational problems. Complex ERP replacements require separate discovery and technical planning.
