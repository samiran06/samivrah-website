Service Terms

Effective: 21 August 2026

Recruitment registration

The ₹3,999 one-time registration fee starts the agreed recruitment activity after payment confirmation. The registration fee is non-refundable after payment. Registration does not guarantee a candidate, interview, selection, joining or continued employment.

Fee after candidate selection

A separate recruitment fee becomes payable when the client confirms selection of a candidate. For each selected candidate, this fee equals one month of that candidate’s agreed salary and is calculated designation-wise. This selection fee is separate from the ₹3,999 registration fee.

Client responsibility

The client is responsible for accurate job details, lawful employment conditions, interviews, final selection, offer terms, identity and background verification, statutory compliance and workplace safety unless otherwise agreed in writing.

Web projects

Project scope, content, revisions, timeline, payment and deployment follow the written proposal. Domain, hosting, email, maintenance, third-party software and paid services are separate unless included in writing. One-time deployment means one deployment to the agreed environment.

Social media

Content volume, platforms and deliverables follow the selected written package. Advertising budget is separate. Rankings, reach, enquiries, leads and sales are not guaranteed.

Payments and changes

Other payment, cancellation and refund terms are confirmed in the applicable written quotation or agreement. Work outside the agreed scope may require a separate fee and timeline.
