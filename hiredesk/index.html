A FEW GOOD QUESTIONS

Can we subscribe now?

This is a product preview. Contact Samivrah to agree a pilot. Secure company accounts, billing and production data storage need to be connected before paid subscriptions open.

Does it send email or WhatsApp updates?

The demo creates editable message drafts. Live email delivery and WhatsApp Business messaging require provider setup, candidate preferences and the relevant messaging configuration.

Does the system reject candidates automatically?

No. Recruiters review candidates and make hiring decisions. Custom screening can organise relevant answers without replacing human judgement.

Can our existing applications be imported?

Migration can be scoped as part of a custom implementation, including field mapping, duplicate review and access permissions.
