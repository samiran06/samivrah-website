Questions, answered

Before you register

Clear expectations support a better recruitment process.

Is the ₹3,999 registration fee refundable?

No. The one-time ₹3,999 recruitment registration fee is non-refundable after payment.

What fee is charged after selection?

After the client confirms selection, a separate recruitment fee equal to one month of each selected candidate’s agreed salary is charged designation-wise.

Does registration guarantee a joining?

No. Candidate availability, interest, interviews, verification, offer conditions and final decisions affect the outcome.

What information should I provide?

Share the role, city, number of openings, salary, shift, benefits, experience and preferred joining date.
