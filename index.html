GOOD QUESTIONS / CLEAR ANSWERS

Before we
get started.

What kinds of businesses do you work with?

Our services are designed for startups, consultants, local businesses and service companies that need a professional website, consistent social content or clearer operations.

What does a website project include?

We agree on the pages, design, enquiry journey, integrations, revisions and handover before starting. Domain, hosting, paid tools and ongoing maintenance are separate unless included in your proposal.

Can we work together remotely?

Yes. Requirements, reviews and handover can be coordinated remotely. Share your location, preferred time zone and project needs so we can agree on a workable schedule.

Will a new website guarantee enquiries or rankings?

No. Clear design and relevant content support discovery and enquiries, but results also depend on demand, traffic, your offer and follow-up. We define the project deliverables rather than promise guaranteed sales or rankings.
